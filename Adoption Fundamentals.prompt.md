# Change Management Fundamentals
## Driving Adoption at Scale

---

# Why Adoption Matters

- Technology and process investments fail without adoption
- ROI is only realized when people actually change behavior
- Adoption = Value Realization
- Without adoption, you have shelfware and wasted resources

---

# The Adoption Equation

**Adoption at Scale = Individual Change × Organizational Alignment**

- Organizations don't change—people do
- Successful change happens one person at a time
- Scale requires systematic approach to individual transitions

---

# The Human Side of Change

> "Change is hard because people overestimate the value of what they have and underestimate the value of what they may gain."
> — James Belasco

---

# Introducing ADKAR

A model for individual change by Prosci

- **A**wareness
- **D**esire
- **K**nowledge
- **A**bility
- **R**einforcement

---

# Awareness

**"I understand why change is needed"**

- Understanding the nature of the change
- Why the change is happening
- The risk of not changing

**Barrier signs:** "Why are we doing this?" "This came out of nowhere"

---

# Desire

**"I want to participate and support the change"**

- Personal motivation to engage
- Understanding "What's in it for me?"
- Willingness to support the change

**Barrier signs:** "I don't see the point" "The old way works fine"

---

# Knowledge

**"I know how to change"**

- Understanding how to change
- Knowledge of new processes, tools, behaviors
- Training and information needed

**Barrier signs:** "I don't know how to do this" "No one trained me"

---

# Ability

**"I can implement the change"**

- Demonstrated capability
- Turning knowledge into action
- Practice and time to develop skills

**Barrier signs:** "I tried but it didn't work" "It's too complicated"

---

# Reinforcement

**"I will sustain the change"**

- Sustaining the change over time
- Recognition and rewards
- Preventing regression to old behaviors

**Barrier signs:** "No one notices anyway" "We're slipping back"

---

# ADKAR is Sequential

- Must address each element in order
- Can't skip ahead—each builds on the previous
- Barriers at any point stall the entire change
- Diagnose where people are stuck, then target that element

---

# The Frozen Middle

**The 68% who determine success or failure**

- ~16% are early adopters—they'll change readily
- ~16% are resistors—they may never fully adopt
- ~68% are waiting and watching
- Your adoption strategy must unlock this middle majority

---

# Eight Levers for Adoption at Scale

1. Executive Sponsorship
2. Manager Coaching
3. Community Champions
4. Communications
5. Training
6. Resistance Management
7. Measurement
8. Rewards & Recognition

---

# Lever 1: Executive Sponsorship

**Builds: Awareness + Desire**

- Visible, active leadership commitment
- Communicates the "why" from the top
- Signals organizational priority
- Coalition of sponsors across the organization

**At scale:** Sponsor network that cascades messaging through layers

---

# Lever 2: Manager Coaching

**Builds: Desire + Ability + Reinforcement**

- Managers are the #1 influence on employee change
- Direct conversations about expectations
- Day-to-day support and accountability
- Removes barriers for their teams

**At scale:** Equip people managers as change agents for the 68%

---

# Lever 3: Community Champions

**Builds: Awareness + Desire + Knowledge**

- Peer advocates embedded in teams
- Credible voices at the ground level
- Answer questions, model behaviors
- Bridge between leadership and frontline

**At scale:** Champion network creates local change momentum

---

# Lever 4: Communications

**Builds: Awareness + Desire**

- Right message, right audience, right time
- Multiple channels and repetition
- Two-way—listen as much as broadcast
- Address the "why" before the "how"

**At scale:** Segmented, sustained campaigns that reach everyone

---

# Lever 5: Training

**Builds: Knowledge + Ability**

- Skills and knowledge transfer
- Multiple modalities (self-paced, instructor-led, hands-on)
- Just-in-time, not just-in-case
- Ongoing, not one-time

**At scale:** Accessible, role-based learning paths

---

# Lever 6: Resistance Management

**Builds: Desire + Reinforcement**

- Anticipate and address objections
- Listen to understand root causes
- Convert resistors into advocates when possible
- Persistent resistance requires escalation

**At scale:** Proactive identification and targeted intervention

---

# Lever 7: Measurement

**Builds: Reinforcement + Awareness**

- Track adoption metrics, not just deployment
- Leading indicators (usage, engagement) and lagging (outcomes)
- Make progress visible
- Use data to target interventions

**At scale:** Dashboards that drive action across the organization

---

# Lever 8: Rewards & Recognition

**Builds: Desire + Reinforcement**

- Celebrate wins publicly
- Align incentives with desired behaviors
- Recognize effort, not just results
- Create positive peer pressure

**At scale:** Systematic recognition programs that reach the 68%

---

# Mapping Levers to ADKAR

| ADKAR Element | Primary Levers |
|---------------|----------------|
| Awareness | Sponsorship, Communications, Champions |
| Desire | Sponsorship, Manager Coaching, Resistance Mgmt, Rewards |
| Knowledge | Training, Champions, Communications |
| Ability | Training, Manager Coaching, Champions |
| Reinforcement | Measurement, Rewards, Manager Coaching |

---

# Unlocking the Frozen Middle

- The 68% need **consistent signals** from multiple levers
- Sponsorship alone won't move them—they watch their managers and peers
- Champions create **social proof** at the team level
- Measurement makes adoption **visible and expected**
- Rewards shift adoption from optional to valued

---

# Key Takeaway

**Adoption at scale requires orchestrating all eight levers to move individuals through ADKAR—especially the 68% who are waiting to see if this change is real.**
